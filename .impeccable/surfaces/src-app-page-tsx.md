## Direction contract

THESIS: A studio site drawn like a design file on a white artboard. Giant expanded uppercase type, purple construction lines with nodes and handles, and inset rounded panels that change ground as you read. It refuses the dark agency reel and the "team of five" pitch: it sells what gets made and the launch it leads to.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, deep aubergine ink #1A0B2E for text and the dark panels, Eve purple #8800FF for type accents, lines, handles and the main button, pale lilac #F3EFFA panels. Archivo expanded uppercase for display, Geist for reading. Hairline 1px purple strokes, square white nodes, 32px panel corners, pill buttons.

STORY: Read "From idea to launch day" and what the studio makes, see the gap it closes, browse the work, watch a sketch become a launched app, scan services with starting prices, meet the people as pixel figures, type what you are launching, read questions, reach the outlined name with the crew sitting on it and book.
